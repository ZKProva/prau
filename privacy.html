Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 August 2026

In short: Prau has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. Speech, text, photos and documents are recognized and translated on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere by the app. The only data that can ever leave the device is what is strictly required to process an in-app purchase, if you make one — and it never includes what you say or translate.

1. Who we are

Prau is an iPhone app for translating conversations, text, photos and PDF documents. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device and what, if anything, leaves it. Where this policy says “we”, it means the developer of Prau published on the App Store. Contact: alexpan77705@gmail.com.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer accounts or registration.
  • We do not run analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties.
  • The app has no servers of its own; the content you translate is not uploaded, stored or read by us.

3. Processing on your iPhone

All core features run on the device using Apple frameworks built into iOS:

  • Speech recognition — Apple’s on-device speech recognition. The app explicitly requires on-device recognition; voice input is only offered for languages where recognition can run offline.
  • Translation — Apple’s Translation framework, running on the device. Language packs are downloaded once by iOS from Apple when you confirm the system prompt; that download is a communication between your device and Apple and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. After the download, translation works with no internet connection.
  • Photos and PDFs — text is recognized on the device (Apple Vision). Images and documents you pick are not copied outside the app and are not uploaded.
  • Speech output — Apple’s on-device text-to-speech.
  • Conversation summaries — generated on the device by Apple Intelligence on iPhones and languages that support it. Nothing is sent to a server for this.

4. Data stored on your device

  • History — your conversations (original text and translation) are saved in the app’s private storage on the iPhone so you can search, reopen and export them. You can delete any conversation, or all of them, in History. Deleting the app deletes the history.
  • Settings — language pair, “my language”, speech rate and similar preferences.
  • Diagnostic log — a small rolling log of timings, error codes and language-pair codes (for example “fr→de”), kept in the app’s storage to help diagnose problems. It never contains what you say or translate, and it is never transmitted anywhere. Similar entries are written to the iOS system log on your device only.

iCloud backups made by iOS may include the app’s data, under your Apple Account and Apple’s terms; the app itself does not sync anything.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — to hear and recognize speech, on the device.
  • Camera — to translate text from photos and scanned documents.
  • Choosing photos or files uses the standard iOS pickers; the app receives only the items you select.

You can change any permission at any time in iOS Settings; the corresponding feature simply stops working until you allow it again.

6. Sharing and export

When you copy, share or export a conversation (TXT/PDF), the content goes only where you send it — to the app or person you choose in the iOS share sheet. That transfer is under your control and outside Prau.

7. In-app purchases

Prau may offer paid plans (subscriptions or a one-time purchase). Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. To check whether a purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service acting as our processor. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated identifier, purchase and subscription status, and technical data such as device type, iOS version and IP address. It never receives your speech, text, photos or history. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. If the app has no paid features yet, none of this data is sent.

8. Children

Prau is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

9. Your rights

Because the app keeps your data on your own device and we do not collect it, you exercise your rights directly: view, export or delete your history in the app, or delete the app. For anything related to a purchase, contact us or Apple. You can also contact us with any question about this policy.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date. Material changes (for example, adding a new data recipient) will also be described in the app’s update notes.
