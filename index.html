How it works

Three steps, then it just works — anywhere.

  1. Pick a pair of languages

    Tap the language buttons at the top. The first time, iOS offers to download the packs — confirm, and after that translation never needs a network.

  2. Tap the microphone and speak

    Prau recognizes your words on the device, translates them and reads the translation aloud. The other person answers and hears their reply in their own language.

  3. Keep the conversation

    Every conversation is saved automatically. Search it, reopen it, export it to TXT or PDF, share it. This part is free, always.

Set up once, before the trip

Two things live inside iOS, not inside Prau. Do them at home on Wi-Fi — after that everything works with no network at all.

  1. Language packs for translation

    Open Prau and pick your pair. iOS shows the “Required downloads” sheet — tap the arrow next to each language. Roughly 50–100 MB per language, once. Later you can add packs in Prau → Settings → Languages.

  2. Offline dictation for your voice

    iPhone Settings → General → Keyboard → Dictation Languages. Add each language you will speak. If a language is missing from that list, add its keyboard once (Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard) — dictation follows automatically. iOS downloads the model in the background, usually within a day, fastest on Wi-Fi while charging. In Prau → Settings → Languages a green microphone means the language is ready.

  3. Headphones — optional

    Connect AirPods or any headphones and choose “My language” in Prau Settings. Your translation plays quietly in your ear; the other person hears theirs from the phone speaker.

Text and photo translation don't need dictation at all — they work as soon as the packs are in.

Support

The microphone is crossed out for a language

Voice input needs the offline dictation model that iOS installs for that language. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Dictation Languages: if the language is listed, iOS is already downloading the model in the background (usually within a day, best on Wi-Fi while charging). If it is not listed, add the keyboard for that language once — dictation switches on by itself. Until then, text and photo translation work fully.

The system 'Required downloads' sheet appears

That is Apple's Translation framework asking for the language packs. Tap the arrow next to each language; the download does not start until you tap. Once done, translation works offline forever.

Translation is slow or the translator isn't responding

This happens on the iOS side, usually right after a language pack was installed, and clears within a minute. If it keeps happening, restart the iPhone.

I paid, but Pro is not active

Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they restore on any of your iPhones. If it still fails, write to us with the App Store receipt.

How do I cancel the subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Prau. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Conversation summaries additionally need Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and newer) and a supported system language.
